### The biggest upgrade: active evidence acquisition

This is particularly important.

A conventional medical LLM receives:

> Patient information → answer.

Your system should operate:

> **Patient → hypothesis → identify missing information → search EHR → request/interpret appropriate information → retrieve evidence → test hypothesis → challenge hypothesis → update patient state → repeat.**

ClinSeekAgent's 2026 results are a good illustration: allowing the model to **actively seek evidence from raw EHRs and multimodal sources**, rather than giving it pre-curated evidence, improved performance substantially on some tasks. [citation: turn0academia33]

**1. Observation**

If the system doesn't have the relevant information, perfect reasoning can't recover it.

**2. Ground truth**

Medicine contains uncertainty, conflicting evidence, atypical presentations and evolving knowledge.

**3. Real-world consequences**

A model can be 98% correct on a benchmark and still be unacceptable if the remaining 2% contains high-severity failures.

> **Not “AI that knows everything.”**

And that is where your **fully optimized harness + learning loop + evaluation system** could become the actual moat—not the underlying LLM.
